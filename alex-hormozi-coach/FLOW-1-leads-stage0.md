# FLOW 1 — Stage 0: No business yet → get your first customers
### Source: $100M Leads (see REF-leads.md)

For the person with a skill or an idea but zero/low revenue, no audience, no ad budget. The goal of this whole stage: **first paying customer, then first five.** Don't talk about churn, CAC, or money models here — it'll just freeze them.

Run the flows in this order: 1.1 → 1.2 → 1.3 → (1.4 as needed) → 1.5. Flows 1.6–1.9 are the four Core Four channels; for a beginner, prescribe Warm Outreach (1.6) first and only add others once it's maxed.

---

## FLOW 1.1 — Pick who you serve (the market)
**When:** they're vague about who it's for, or niche-hopping.
**Steps:**
1. Ask: "Who, specifically, do you help — and what result do you get them?"
2. Score their market against the **4 indicators**: massive pain · purchasing power · easy to target · growing. Ask one question per indicator.
3. If any indicator is weak, push them to narrow to a subgroup inside Health, Wealth, or Relationships. ("Riches are in the niches.")
4. Lock a one-line statement: "I help **[specific who]** get **[dream outcome]** in **[time]** without **[effort/sacrifice]**."
**Decision branch:** can't name a specific who → make them pick the narrowest group they've already gotten a result for. No result yet → go to 1.3 (start free) to manufacture one.
**Done when:** the one-line statement exists and passes the 4 indicators.

---

## FLOW 1.2 — Build a lead magnet (only if needed)
**When:** the offer is expensive or needs explaining; weak opt-in. **Skip if** they can just advertise the core offer directly — try that first.
**Steps (the 7):**
1. **Pick the narrow problem** to solve (use Problem-Solution Cycle: solving it reveals the next problem, which your core offer solves).
2. **Pick the type:** (a) reveal their problem / diagnosis, (b) sample or trial, (c) one step of a multi-step process.
3. **Pick the delivery:** software · information · service · physical.
4. **Name it** — write the headline first ("80 cents of your ad dollar"). A/B two headlines.
5. **Make it easy to consume** — offer multiple formats.
6. **Make it genuinely great** — "give away the secret, sell the implementation."
7. **Add a CTA:** what to do next + a reason to act now.
**Done when:** a free thing exists that solves one narrow problem and points to the paid offer.

---

## FLOW 1.3 — Your First Five Clients (start free → flip to paid)
**When:** literally zero paying customers, or a brand-new offer/line.
**Steps:**
1. Reframe: "Make the first five free. You probably suck right now — free buys you reps, forgiveness, feedback, and testimonials."
2. Deliver the free script: *"I'm only taking five people so I can get you brag-worthy results — for free — as long as you (1) use it, (2) give me feedback, (3) leave a killer review if it earns one. Fair?"*
3. Deliver hard. Collect feedback and a review from each.
4. **Litmus test to start charging:** the moment people start *referring*, start charging.
5. Apply the **+5 rule:** free → 80% off for the next 5 → 60% → 40% → keep raising ~20% every 5 sales until price stings a little. (Real urgency: prices genuinely rise.)
6. **Cash twist (optional):** "Would you rather pay less today, or get all your money back?" Pay-less = plan/no guarantee; all-back = prepay + guarantee. Most pick prepay-with-guarantee → more cash up front.
**Done when:** five results delivered, testimonials collected, and pricing has begun climbing.

---

## FLOW 1.4 — Show proof (raise belief)
**When:** close rate lags because they don't believe it'll work for *them*.
**Steps:** include one or more of the **11 proofs** in the offer/pitch: (1) your own story, (2) testimonials from people like them, (3) volume of reviews, (4) certifications, (5) stats/research, (6) experts vouching, (7) a new mechanism they haven't failed with, (8) celebrity, (9) guarantee, (10) describe their pain so well "he gets me," (11) demonstrate the outcome live/recorded.
**Done when:** at least 2–3 proof types are visible in the pitch (10 testimonials is the highest-leverage single move — chase those first).

---

## FLOW 1.5 — Pick ONE Core Four channel and commit
**When:** the offer + proof exist; now drive volume.
**Steps:**
1. Ask: "Do you have more time or more money right now?"
2. Route: more time → **Post Content (1.7)**; more money → **Cold Outreach (1.8) or Paid Ads (1.9)**. Either way, **start with Warm Outreach (1.6)** for the first customers.
3. Set a **Rule of 100**: 100 primary actions/day for 100 days. Pick the one action and the start date.
4. "You only need ONE channel to eat. Pick one. Max it out before adding another."
**Done when:** one channel chosen, daily number set, start date committed.

---

## FLOW 1.6 — Warm Outreach (start here; cheapest, most reliable)
**When:** brand-new, no audience, no money — or re-engaging a list.
**Steps (the 10):**
1. Build the list (phone + email + every social — "your first thousand leads").
2. Pick the platform where you have the most contacts.
3. Personalize the greeting with something you know about them.
4. Reach out to **100 people/day**, up to 3 times until they respond.
5. Warm them with **ACA: Acknowledge → Compliment → Ask** another question.
6. Invite their friends / make the offer.
7. Make the easiest offer in the world — **free** (run FLOW 1.3).
8. Exhaust one platform top-to-bottom, then move to the next.
9. Start charging once referrals come (+5 rule).
10. Keep the list warm with value + the "**Are you still looking to ___?**" 9-word email.
**Benchmark:** 100 reachouts → ~20 replies → ~4 take the free offer → ~1 paying. "This alone can hit $100k/year."
**Done when:** running 100/day and the first paying customers have landed.

---

## FLOW 1.7 — Post Content (one-to-many, warm)
**When:** more time than money; wants compounding inbound.
**Steps:**
1. Build each post as a **Content Unit: Hook → Retain → Reward.**
   - **Hook:** topic (far past / recent past / present / trending / manufactured) + headline (recency, relevancy, celebrity, proximity, conflict, unusual, ongoing) + platform-matched format.
   - **Retain:** use lists, steps, or stories; embed open questions.
   - **Reward:** value per second — "no such thing as too long, only too boring."
2. Make all content **for strangers** (so it gets shared).
3. Monetize with **Give-Give-Give until they Ask**: give in public, ask in private. One CTA at a time.
4. Scale depth-then-width (max one platform, then add) or width-then-depth.
5. Apply **Rule of 100**: 100 minutes of content/day.
**Done when:** posting daily at volume; audience (not post count) is growing.

---

## FLOW 1.8 — Cold Outreach (one-to-one, cold)
**When:** more money than time; higher-ticket; wants predictable private lead flow.
**Steps:**
1. Get a way to contact strangers (lists).
2. **What to say = Personalize + Big Fast Value.** Make it look warm (1–3 personal facts + compliment); "give away the farm" in under 30 seconds — give something they'd actually pay for. Write below a 3rd-grade reading level.
3. **Volume (3 levers):** automate delivery; automate distribution (trade some personalization for scale); follow up more times, more ways (email → call "to follow up on my email" → re-run the list in 3–6 months).
4. **Count in 100s:** never set a daily goal under 100; don't stop for 100 days.
**Benchmark:** ~3% of an email list becomes engaged; phone ~20% pickup × ~25% take. Healthy when LTGP ≥ 3× CAC.
**Done when:** 100/day going out and engaged-lead rate is tracked.

---

## FLOW 1.9 — Paid Ads (one-to-many, cold; fastest scale)
**When:** has money to risk, tracking in place, LTGP comfortably > CAC.
**Steps:**
1. **Where:** pick a platform where competitors already advertise.
2. **Target:** lookalikes from a customer list + filters (age, income, interest, location).
3. **Make the ad = Call-Out + Value + CTA.**
   - Call-Out (most important): labels, yes-questions, if-then statements, contrast, the scene. Make 30+ call-outs × ~10 ads = 300 variations.
   - Value = What (8 elements) / Who (status across their people) / When (past-present-future).
   - CTA: exact next step + urgency/scarcity/bonus.
4. **Landing page** matches the ad.
5. **Money in 3 phases:** Track money → Lose money (budget ~2× the 30-day cash per customer to test; expect more losers than winners; 10× down on winners) → Print money (reverse-budget from your customer goal, pad 20%).
**Metric:** LTGP:CAC ≥ 3:1. Below 3× industry CAC → fix the business model; above → fix the ads. Aim for **Client-Financed Acquisition** (make back acquisition + fulfillment cost in 30 days).
**Done when:** tracking live, winners scaling, ratio ≥ 3:1.

---

## Stage 0 graduation
Promote to Stage 1/2 when revenue becomes consistent and the constraint shifts from "how do I get anyone" to "how do I package/price this" (→ FLOW-2) or "how do I monetize/scale what's working" (→ FLOW-3).
